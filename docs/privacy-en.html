Local game data

Dart Club is a scoring tool for darts matches. Player names, profiles, scores, accuracy statistics, preferences and unfinished games are stored only on your device for the features you choose to use. No online account is required. AJS Studio does not receive this data and does not operate a profile server for this app.

No advertising or usage measurement

This edition displays no ads and includes no Google Mobile Ads/AdMob, UMP consent SDK, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. The app does not transmit advertising identifiers, IP addresses, location, diagnostics or usage events to AJS Studio or an advertising service. It requests no network, location, camera or microphone permission.

All ages

Dart Club is intended for all ages, including children. It collects no ages or dates of birth. Children’s profiles remain local, just like other profiles, and are not used for ad targeting. An adult should supervise younger players using real darts.

Backups and sharing you request

You can export or restore a JSON backup and share a result image. Android lets you choose the file, destination app or recipient. These files are not sent to an AJS Studio server. Any service you select to store or receive them applies its own privacy rules. Android system backups depend on your device settings and backup provider.

Retention and deletion

Game data remains local until you delete it or reset the app. Android → App info → Storage → Clear data removes all local app data. Export anything you want to keep first. Delete previously exported files and system backups separately. AJS Studio has no server copy of your local scores.

Email support

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to answer your request. This relies on AJS Studio’s legitimate interest in providing support or on applicable legal obligations. Messages are kept as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations, then deleted when no longer needed. Email providers process them to deliver the messages. Never send passwords or login codes.

External links and hosting

Opening this policy, ajsstudio.fr or an email uses another app selected through Android. Those services may process technical information about your visit or message under their own policies. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads no tracking tool added by AJS Studio. The AJS Studio website has a separate privacy policy.

For personal data processed by AJS Studio, contact contact@ajsstudio.fr to exercise applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability rights. Identify Dart Club and your request. Requests are answered as soon as possible, normally within one month, subject to justified legal extensions for complex requests. Changes

This notice applies to the ad-free 0.15.0 edition. If advertising or remote services are introduced later, the policy and privacy declarations will be updated before activation.
